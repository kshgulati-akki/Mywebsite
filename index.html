Our story

Paan has always been more than a mouth freshener — it's hospitality. The Paan Box brings that tradition to your table with carefully sourced leaves, house-made fillings and packaging worth gifting.

Every paan is rolled fresh on the day of delivery. No shortcuts, no artificial flavours — just the taste you remember.

  • 🍃 Sourced leaves, selected daily
  • 🌹 House-made gulkand & fillings
  • 📦 Premium, reusable boxes
  • 🚚 Local delivery available
