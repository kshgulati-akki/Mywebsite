# JobReach: copy-paste prompts

**1. Start**
`Start the JobReach interview. Ask me one question at a time.`

**2. Profile rewrite**
`PROFILE: here is my current headline and About: [paste]. Rewrite them for [target role] in [city]. Give 3 headline options.`

**3. Find the right people**
`TARGETS: give me 5 LinkedIn search strings to find hiring managers and recruiters for [target role] in [city/industry].`

**4. Score a person**
`Score this person for me 0-10 and say contact now, nurture or skip: [paste their headline and About].`

**5. Write the message**
`MESSAGE: Name [x], role [y], company [z], something real I noticed: [detail]. My caption: [your caption]. Give me 2 versions, max 60 words.`

**6. Follow-ups**
`FOLLOW-UP: I messaged [name] on [date], no reply. Write the Day 4 message.`

**7. Comment to get noticed**
`COMMENTS: here is a post by a hiring manager: [paste]. Draft 3 thoughtful comments from me.`

**8. Weekly review**
`WEEKLY REVIEW: I sent [n], got [n] replies, [n] calls. What should I change this week?`

**Rule:** you send every message yourself, 10-15 a day max.
